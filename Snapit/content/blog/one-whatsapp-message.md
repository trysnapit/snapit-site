---
title: What happens after one WhatsApp message
date: "2026-10-11"
summary: One photo or PDF on WhatsApp is read, posted with the original document, answered in the same chat, and checked for a duplicate.
---

After one WhatsApp message, Snapit reads the invoice, posts the bill with the original document, replies in the same chat with the total, and flags the message if that invoice was already booked.

1. Send a photo or PDF on WhatsApp.
2. Snapit reads the supplier, invoice number, date, tax, and total.
3. The bill and the original document post.
4. The same chat replies with the total.

Snapit reads these fields from a photo or PDF:

- Supplier
- Invoice number
- Date
- Line items
- Tax
- Total
- Duplicate check

It checks what is already posted, so nothing goes in twice. Branch staff get the total back and a duplicate warning. Finance sees every branch, posted or flagged.
