---
title: Why retyping invoices costs more than the typing
date: "2026-10-10"
summary: Retyping costs more than the time it takes, because the numbers, the cash, the records, and the people all take the hit.
---

Retyping invoices costs more than the typing. Invoices arrive from WhatsApp, email, PDFs, and paper, and someone retypes every one. It is slow, and amounts get entered wrong. Beyond the typing time, these are the costs.

- **Wrong numbers.** Wrong numbers in reports and tax filings.
- **Paying twice.** Cash paid out twice.
- **Lost invoices.** Expenses that never get recorded.
- **Books behind.** No view of what is owed.
- **No paper trail.** Audits become a document hunt.
- **Wasted talent.** Finance staff stuck on data entry.
